# Personal Historical Note

Supratim Sanyal recalls personally interacting with Željko Jurić and
Samir Ribić around the time they initially released Warajevo.

Their country was passing through a period of severe hardship. During those
exchanges, they described the price of a loaf of bread as being approximately
the same as the price of a bicycle. Sanyal remembers them explaining that
concentrating on the emulator helped them continue through those dark times.

This account is a personal recollection. It is preserved here as context about
the people and circumstances behind Warajevo, not as independently verified
economic or historical documentation.
