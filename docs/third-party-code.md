## Consequences

1. Do not apply one blanket licence header to every imported file.
2. Preserve original notices exactly.
3. Do not claim that the official site's general GPL statement resolves every
   third-party component.
4. Consider replacing third-party components in a modernized branch.
5. Keep archival and modernized distributions clearly separated.

See `LICENSING.md` for the fuller licensing evidence.
